Co-op maintenance bundles three things a condo owner pays separately: building upkeep (staff, repairs, amenities), a per-share portion of the building's underlying mortgage (many co-op buildings carry one, unlike most condos), and a per-share portion of the building's property tax bill. Because the tax and mortgage-interest portions can be deductible, maintenance isn't a purely apples-to-apples comparison against a condo's [common charges](/glossary/common-charges/) plus separate property taxes — a real comparison has to account for that deductibility.

## Typical citywide range

Roughly $750/month in Queens, $900/month in Brooklyn, and $1,500-$1,800/month in Manhattan, per market data cited on this site's [Co-op Calculator](/coop/), which uses $1,200/month as a mid-range citywide default — always verify the actual figure from a building's financials or a current listing. Maintenance also directly determines your [DTI](/glossary/debt-to-income-ratio-dti/) budget for the mortgage payment itself, since a board caps the combined total, not maintenance and mortgage separately. See [co-op vs condo costs](/guides/coop-vs-condo-nyc-costs/) for the full comparison.
