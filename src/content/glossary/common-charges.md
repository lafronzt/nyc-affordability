Common charges are the monthly fee condo owners pay toward building staff, insurance, repairs, and shared amenities — doorman, elevator, gym, roof, and so on. Unlike a co-op's [maintenance](/glossary/maintenance-coop/), common charges do **not** include a share of an underlying building mortgage (condos generally don't have one) and do **not** include property taxes, which condo owners pay separately and directly.

## Typical citywide range

Full-service Manhattan buildings often run $1,500-$3,000+/month; boutique buildings in Brooklyn or Queens are often $500-$900/month. This site's [Condo Calculator](/condo/) uses $1,000/month as an illustrative citywide starting default — always verify a specific building's actual common charges from its offering plan or a current listing, since the range is wide. See [co-op vs condo costs](/guides/coop-vs-condo-nyc-costs/) for how common charges plus separate property taxes compare to a co-op's all-in maintenance figure.
