## Important: these figures cover a broader zone than "Harlem"

We could not find a Harlem-specific median from a stable, dated source. Both figures below come from broker-defined market zones that bundle Harlem together with neighboring areas:

- The rent figure covers Elliman's "Northern Manhattan" rental zone.
- The sale figure covers Brown Harris Stevens' "Upper Manhattan" zone — everything roughly north of 96th Street on the East Side and 110th Street on the West Side, which bundles Harlem with Washington Heights, Inwood, and Morningside Heights.

Washington Heights and Inwood are generally lower-priced than central Harlem, which likely pulls this zone's median below what you'd actually see on listings in Harlem proper. Treat this as a directional starting point, not a precise Harlem number.
