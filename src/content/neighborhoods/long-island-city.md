## What these numbers actually cover

The sale price is genuinely LIC-specific, from Corcoran's dedicated Long Island City & Astoria report, notably higher than Astoria's, consistent with LIC's newer-construction, higher-rise condo stock. The rent figure is not neighborhood-specific; it comes from Elliman's broader "Northwest Queens" rental zone, which combines LIC with Astoria, Sunnyside, and Woodside. We could not find an LIC-only rent median from a stable, dated source, so this page uses the same zone-wide rent figure as our [Astoria page](/neighborhoods/astoria/). In practice, LIC's newer towers likely command a rent premium over that blended zone average.
