## What these numbers actually cover

The sale price is genuinely Astoria-specific, from Corcoran's dedicated Long Island City & Astoria report. The rent figure is not; it comes from Elliman's broader "Northwest Queens" rental zone, which combines Astoria with Long Island City, Sunnyside, and Woodside. We could not find an Astoria-only rent median from a stable, dated source, so this page uses the same zone-wide rent figure as our [Long Island City page](/neighborhoods/long-island-city/), so treat the rent number as directional for the area, not Astoria-exact.
